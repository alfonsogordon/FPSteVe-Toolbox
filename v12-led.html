V12 Max RGB LED Script

HelloRadioSky V12 Max · EdgeTX · Custom RGB lighting

RGBSTKS.lua

Custom lighting for six RGB LEDs, with throttle-controlled brightness and colour changes driven by your stick movements.

Download Lua script ↓View source ↗

Features

  • Six RGB LEDs with throttle-reactive fill and brightness
  • OUT (centre out), IN (outside in) and SCROLL (left to right) fill patterns
  • Roll, pitch and yaw influence the colour palette
  • Adjustable idle glow, maximum brightness and colour strength
  • Optional stick colour changes restricted to the filled LEDs

Installation

  1. Download RGBSTKS.lua.
  2. Copy it to /SCRIPTS/FUNCTIONS/RGBSTKS.lua on the EdgeTX SD card.
  3. In your radio's Special Functions, assign the Lua script to an available function slot and enable it.
  4. Check the LEDs respond to throttle and stick inputs before normal use.

The script calls setRGBLedColor() and applyRGBLedColors(); your EdgeTX build must support these functions.

Customise the effects

Edit these values near the top of the Lua file:

  • FILL_MODE — "OUT", "IN" or "SCROLL"
  • IDLE_GLOW — brightness at zero throttle (default 25)
  • MAX_BRIGHTNESS — maximum brightness (default 255)
  • COLOUR_POWER — colour contrast (default 1.35)
  • STICK_COLOUR_AMOUNT — how strongly sticks change colours (default 0.45)
  • STICK_FOLLOWS_FILL — limit stick colour effects to lit LEDs (default false)
